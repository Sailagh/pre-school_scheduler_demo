How long does a good schedule take?

The solver made one week with different work budgets. Each result was checked with the planner's own rules. "Satisfactory": nothing under Att kontrollera and everyone within 15 minutes of their contract hours.

The computers side by side

Median seconds per week at each work budget, all cores. LEVI: Intel(R) Core(TM) i9-14900HX, 32 logical CPUs; cloud-xeon-4cpu: Intel(R) Xeon(R) Processor @ 2.80GHz, 4 logical CPUs. The solver runs one search per logical CPU, so the same budget is spread over as many searches as the computer has logical CPUs: it is not the same work, and more searches are not always faster.
