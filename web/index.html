Showing it

  1. In the planner, press ▶ Demo for the six-step sick-report story: Anna reports sick, the shift goes to substitutes, Sara takes it, the principal confirms.
  2. Open the planner and the phone app in two tabs of the same browser. A sick report or swap sent from the phone shows up in the planner's Notifications and Sick cover right away.
  3. On a phone, open the phone app and add it to the home screen for a full-screen app feel.
  4. To start over, go to Settings › General › Start over in the planner.

This is a click-through demo with made-up people. Changes are saved only in the browser you use, so each viewer gets their own copy. The schedule solver (OR-Tools) and shared data between devices come in the next step.
